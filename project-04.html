About This Property

This unique 6-acre estate offers a rare combination: close proximity to the highway for easy access, yet nestled behind a natural stream that creates a private, tranquil setting. The stream provides year-round water access — ideal for irrigation, livestock, or creating a serene water feature for a weekend retreat.

The property presents exceptional flexibility for development. Whether you envision a boutique eco-resort, a wellness retreat center, an organic orchard, or a private family farmhouse, the natural landscape and water feature provide a compelling canvas.

With adjacent land available, there's a rare opportunity to expand the estate to 25 acres — creating a substantial holding suitable for larger-scale agricultural operations, a destination farm-stay, or a private nature sanctuary. The highway proximity ensures the property remains accessible for guests or produce transport, while the stream creates a natural buffer for privacy and ambiance.
