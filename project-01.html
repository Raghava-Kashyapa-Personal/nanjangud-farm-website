About This Property

This flagship 7-acre estate represents the gold standard for income-generating agricultural investments in the Nanjangud corridor. The property features a mature arecanut plantation that delivers consistent annual harvests, supplemented by strategic intercropping zones with coconut, banana, and fruit trees for diversified income streams.

Water security is guaranteed by a dedicated 3.5 lakh liter storage pond with integrated irrigation infrastructure, ensuring year-round cultivation capability regardless of monsoon variations. The property includes cattle and sheep husbandry operations, adding both income and natural fertilizer production for sustainable farm operations.

A professional on-site caretaker and operations team handle all day-to-day management, from planting and maintenance to harvest and sales. Investors receive detailed income reporting and can expect turnkey operations with minimal involvement required. The clear title documentation and verified RTC records are ready for legal review.
