About This Property

This prime residential farm plot is strategically located on Mananthavadi Road in Heggadadevanakote (HD Kote), one of Karnataka's emerging investment corridors. The property sits just 50 meters from the main road, ensuring excellent accessibility and visibility.

With surrounding land prices upwards of Rs 4Cr per acre, this property offers significant appreciation potential. Of the total 32 guntas, 15.5G has already been booked, with 16.5G still available for purchase. The minimum purchase requirement is 5 guntas, making it accessible for a range of investment sizes.

HD Kote's proximity to Mysore and its growing infrastructure development make this an ideal opportunity for investors looking for residential farm plots with strong future value appreciation.
