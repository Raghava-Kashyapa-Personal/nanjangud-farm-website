About This Property

This 4-acre arecanut plantation on Chandravadi Road offers an exceptional opportunity for investors seeking both income and appreciation. The plantation was established 3 years ago and is now approaching the harvest-ready stage, expected within 2 years.

The road-facing location provides excellent accessibility and adds significant value to the property. Arecanut is one of Karnataka's most reliable cash crops, with consistent demand and strong market prices.

With professional management and established irrigation systems, this property is positioned to deliver reliable annual income once harvesting begins, while the land itself continues to appreciate in value.
